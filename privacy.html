Privacy Policy

Serpent Playground Word is an offline word-finder snake game.

Effective date: July 12, 2026

Data Collection

The game does not collect, sell, or share personal information. It does not require an account, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or internet permission.

Local Game Progress

The game may save stage progress, stars, coin balance, sound preference, and pace setting locally on the player's device. This information stays on the device and is used only to continue gameplay.

Children

The game is designed as a general word-learning game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third Parties

The Android app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social login, or third-party tracking libraries.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details shown on the app's Google Play listing.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the game changes. The latest version will be published with the game.
