## Full Description

```text
Serpent Playground Word is a mobile word-finder snake game where players guide a snake, collect letter apples, and build hidden English words.

Each stage gives a small letter bank and a secret word target count. Players must control the snake carefully, collect useful letters, avoid hitting the snake body, use clues wisely, earn stars, collect limited coins, and unlock new stages.

Features:
- 50 word-finding stages
- Smooth touch controls for mobile play
- Offline gameplay
- Secret target words revealed after discovery
- Stars, coins, clues, and progress tracking
- Colorful snake, apple letters, sounds, and reactions
- No account required
- No ads or tracking SDKs
```
